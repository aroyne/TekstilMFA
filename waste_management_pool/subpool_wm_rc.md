# Material recycling (WM.RC)

Mechanical or chemical recycling of textiles: abroad, and from 2024 at Norsk Tekstilgjenvinning (Sandefjord). Clipping stock: takes up the mass created when export for recycling (the balance) is clipped at 0

Type: process. Lager: clipping.
